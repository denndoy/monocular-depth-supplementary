# Public media-sharing statement

The researcher reports oral permission from participating adults and the parents/guardians of participating children to use research images/videos **with faces blurred**, including publication on a public GitHub repository, downloading by others, and redistribution **as long as the face blurring remains intact**. The researcher confirms the condition has been implemented.

This is a statement of **reported oral permission**. No signed forms or recorded consent evidence were provided for independent verification. No independent frame-by-frame privacy audit was carried out during this release. Face blurring does not ensure anonymity because of clothing, background, location, or other visual cues.

## Included materials

- Four demo MP4s: Videos 2–4 inference plus an additional Video 1 in-sample demonstration, described as face-blurred and audio-free.
- Eight field-setup/measurement JPG derivatives, described as having EXIF/GPS removed and the person visible in an affected photograph blurred.
- Six paper figures, including a field photo and a camera/3D visualization composite.
- Unblurred source videos and original photographs are not part of this repository.

## Scope and previous license

The reported permission covers **viewing, downloading and sharing the blurred versions while retaining blur**. It is not interpreted as authorization to unblur or identify people, redistribute unblurred originals, or grant a blanket right for arbitrary adaptation/commercial licensing. Broader uses require separate confirmation.

CC BY 4.0 and MIT notices had earlier been included by an AI agent without a final explicit author selection. Their removal from the current branch does **not** retroactively revoke historical grants. See [LICENSE_STATUS.md](LICENSE_STATUS.md). The participant permissions and institutional ethics procedures are separate issues.

Any privacy concern should be referred to the corresponding author.
